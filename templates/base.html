About StockRoom

StockRoom is the practice's shared stock list. Instead of a monthly stocktake, it's kept up to date by whoever's actually using the supplies, so the picture of what's on the shelf is always current.

How it works

Assistants log an item as soon as they use it, no counting sheets, no forms to fill in. StockRoom watches each item's usage rate and tells you when it's likely to run out, rather than waiting for someone to notice a gap on the shelf. The manager then works from a single reorder list grouped by supplier, calls or emails them in a tap, and ticks items off once they're ordered.

Why it's worth using

It catches items before they run out instead of after, saves the team from doing mental maths or keeping a whiteboard tally, and gives a rough sense of what the practice spends on supplies without anyone having to dig through invoices.

Roles

Dental assistants can log what they've used and flag an item as running low, and that's about all — no prices, no supplier calls, no admin screens to wade through, so it stays quick to use with gloves on between patients. Practice managers and owners can do everything assistants can, plus order stock, call or email suppliers, add and edit items, update counts and prices, edit suppliers and their delivery times, and see the Spending page.
