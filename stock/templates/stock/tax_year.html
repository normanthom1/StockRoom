Keeping the paperwork

StockRoom keeps the original file behind every invoice it reads, and holds it for {{ retention_years }} years after the end of the tax year it belongs to, which is what the Tax Administration Act asks for. Nothing is deleted on that date by itself.

StockRoom's servers are outside New Zealand, and Inland Revenue expects business records to be kept in New Zealand unless it has approved otherwise. Keep your own copy of the CSV above and your invoice files, and ask your accountant whether you need anything more.
